2. Account and technical requirements

Mori requires an account. You enter into an agreement for free account access when you complete registration with an email address and password or Google Sign-In. The agreement continues until you close your account as described in section 5.

You need an Android device compatible with the version specified in the Mori listing on Google Play. An internet connection is required for registration, signing in and purchase verification. Purchases require a Google Play account.

Supplying unlawful content through Mori is prohibited.

3. Scope of the service

Mori provides Japanese lessons, character exercises, vocabulary review and reading practice. Features and materials marked as free are available without payment and may include ads.

Optional Mori Plus access includes the features and materials described before purchase. Offline study is available only with Mori Plus, for the content specified in that offer.

5. Subscription cancellation and account closure

To stop future charges, open your Google Play subscriptions, select Mori Plus and cancel renewal before the next billing date. Access normally continues until the end of the paid period.

You can end your account agreement at any time by requesting account deletion using the Mori account deletion instructions. To stop subscription renewal, cancel it separately in Google Play.
